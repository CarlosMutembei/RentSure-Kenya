🔐 Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective: January 2025

We are committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring transparency in how RentSure Kenya uses your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data such as your name, email, phone number, identification details, and payment information. We also store property listings, photos, videos, and interaction data to improve platform safety and accuracy.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to provide rental services, verify landlords and tenants, process escrow payments, prevent fraud, improve search accuracy, and enhance user experience across RentSure Kenya.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information may only be shared with trusted payment providers (e.g. M-Pesa integrations), escrow processors, or legal authorities when required by law.

4. Security & Escrow Protection

We use secure encryption, authentication systems, and escrow-based payment protection. While we apply strong safeguards, no digital system is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Requests can be made through our support team at any time.

6. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, email us at: privacy@rentsurekenya.co.ke

🛡 Your Data is Protected

RentSure Kenya is built on trust, transparency, and secure transactions for every renter and landlord.
