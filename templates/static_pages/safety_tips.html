🚨 Fraud Prevention Guide

Safety Tips – Avoid Rental Scams

RentSure Kenya protects you, but you must also follow these safety guidelines when renting a property.

🚫

Never send money before viewing

Always visit the property physically before any payment, even if the listing looks real.

🛡️

Use RentSure Escrow

Your money is held securely and only released after you confirm the property is valid.

🔍

Verify landlord identity

Check ID, ownership documents, and ensure the landlord matches the property listing.

📸

Compare photos with property

Some scammers reuse images. Always confirm the property matches the listing.

📞

Report suspicious listings

If something feels wrong, report it immediately through RentSure support.

⭐

Check reviews before deciding

Always read tenant feedback and ratings before committing to a property.

⚠️ Trust Your Instincts

If anything feels suspicious, unclear, or rushed — stop the transaction immediately. Trust your gut and walk away.
