How RentSure Works

A premium, secure ecosystem engineered to help you discover and secure your next home in Kenya with zero stress and absolute protection.

Phase 01

Search & Discover

Use our smart filters to seamlessly pinpoint your perfect home. Filter by customized budgets, exact locations, or direct proximity to your workplace across Kenya.

Phase 02

Verified Listings Only

Every property undergoes a strict verification vetting layout. We actively purge duplicate and fraudulent agents, keeping your search 100% genuine.

Phase 03

Secure Escrow Payment

Your deposit and commitment funds are locked securely within our secure escrow network. The landlord only gets compensated once you give the green light.

Phase 04

View, Confirm & Move In

Conduct your physical walkthrough safely. Once you confirm the space matches your exact specifications, release the payment effortlessly from your dashboard.

Phase 05

Review & Build Trust

Leave transparent feedback on your landlord and property. Share your experience to safeguard and guide the rest of the Kenyan rental community.

🛡️ The RentSure Vision

We built RentSure to decisively eliminate rental scams, control unexpected agent exploitation fees, and orchestrate an authentic, transparent real-estate marketplace for Kenya.
